## Longer, softer reactive fleece — 21 October 2026

- Increased coat length from 0.145 to 0.32 scene units and strand curve resolution from five to eight segments. Finer fibers, stronger drape/curl, reduced highlight/root contrast and trimmed eye/keyboard areas give the coat a fuller silhouette while retaining readable features. Framing reserves more room for the long tips.
- Added bounded, damped springs driven by actual body translation/acceleration, quaternion rotation/acceleration and squash. Local motion bends individual strands differently by their root position, producing follow-through. The spring advances once per scene frame using the animation clock, never once per view; paused frames freeze its state. No geometry is rebuilt during movement and no GPU physics targets are added.
- All ten automated tests pass, including opposing rotational lag, squash response, settling, paused-clock behavior and working-pose-driven inertia. TypeScript, optimized native build and strict signature verification pass. Browser pause checks confirm stable pose time and frame count.
- Chrome and native WebKit GPU checks pass for all profiles. In the 256 × 320 fixture the coat changes 15,257 pixels; enabling the body-driven lag changes 10,054 pixels at the same body pose. Every light-field view receives identical spring state and pose time. Synthetic interlacing checks retain zero sampled 8-bit error.
